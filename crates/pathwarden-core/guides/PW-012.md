# PW-012: Trust between forests with weak SID filtering

**Category:** Hygiene
**Typical owner:** AD / identity team

## What this finding means
This trust between forests filters security identifiers (SIDs) only weakly: a **forest** trust
with SID history enabled (TREAT_AS_EXTERNAL, trustAttributes bit 0x40), or an **external** trust
that is not quarantined. Authentication coming from the trusted domain may carry SIDs of your
domain in an account's SID history, and your domain honors those with a RID of 1000 or above.
It still drops the built-in and privileged SIDs: every RID under 1000 (Administrator 500,
krbtgt 502, Domain Admins 512, Schema Admins 518, Enterprise Admins 519), Enterprise Domain
Controllers (S-1-5-9) and the built-in SIDs (S-1-5-32-*, Everyone, NT Authority).

## Why attackers care
An attacker who compromises the trusted domain can forge a Kerberos ticket whose SID history
carries the SID of an account or group of your domain with a RID of 1000 or above, and your
domain grants what that SID holds (Access Token Manipulation: SID-History Injection, MITRE ATT&CK
T1134.005). Domain Admins and Enterprise Admins can't be forged this way, but groups and accounts
you created can: Exchange Windows Permissions, an Entra Connect (MSOL_) account, a custom admin
group. Only rights the forged SID holds directly or through domain-local groups apply; its global
and universal group memberships are not expanded. Where such a SID controls Tier Zero, a breach
of the trusted domain is a breach of yours.

Inside one forest (parent and child domains) none of this filtering applies: whoever controls
one domain controls the forest, which Pathwarden shows as forest takeover, not PW-012.

No public lab test of a real non-quarantined external trust exists; what passes is taken from the
protocol specification (MS-PAC 4.1.2.2) and the forest trust lab work cited below.

## When the collector's flag is the only evidence
On a **forest** trust collected without its trust attributes (SharpHound 2.3, for example),
the only evidence is the collector's `SidFilteringEnabled` flag. That flag only reports
quarantine, which forest trusts don't use, so it normally reads "off" on a forest trust: it
can't tell default filtering from SID history enabled. The finding then says **SID filtering
unknown** (as the Forest view does) but stays High: if SID history is enabled the impact is
forest-wide, and the unknown is no reason to rank it lower, so verify first. SID history may be forgeable across
the trust: if SID history is enabled (TREAT_AS_EXTERNAL, trustAttributes bit 0x40), accounts and
groups with RIDs of 1000 and above pass. To know, collect the trust attributes (RustHound-CE or a
newer SharpHound gives them), or check on a domain controller of the trusting domain:
`(Get-ADTrust -Filter "Target -eq '<TrustedDomain>'").TrustAttributes -band 0x40`. Get-ADTrust
shows TrustAttributes in decimal (72 is 0x48, which includes 0x40), so test the bit rather than
reading the number. Non-zero: SID history is enabled; apply the fix below. 0: the forest trust
filters SIDs; accept the finding as a risk in Settings, with a note.
Where the attributes are known, or the trust is external (whose flag is quarantine itself), the
finding states it as fact and is High: "has SID history enabled (TREAT_AS_EXTERNAL)" on a forest
trust, "is not quarantined" on an external one.

## Before you change anything
- SID history is usually let through on purpose during a **migration**, so migrated accounts keep
  access through their old SIDs in sIDHistory. Confirm with the AD team whether a migration is
  still running.
- Find users who still depend on SID history: `Get-ADUser -Filter * -Properties SIDHistory | Where-Object SIDHistory`.
  Re-permission their resources with their new SIDs before tightening the filter.
- Quarantining an **external** trust also filters SIDs from other domains in the trusted forest.
  Plan for access that crosses those domains.

## Fix
### Option A: Tighten SID filtering on the trust (recommended)
Run on a domain controller of the trusting domain (the domain that grants access), as a
Domain Admin. `<TrustingDomain>` is the domain the finding's fix names (usually the first in its
title; for a record the collector saw as inbound, the second).
1. Open an elevated command prompt.
2. For a **forest** trust, turn SID history off across it so the forest boundary filters again:
   `netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /enablesidhistory:no`.
   Do not quarantine a forest trust: Microsoft says quarantine "should not be applied to forest
   trust relationships".
3. For an **external** trust, quarantine it:
   `netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /quarantine:yes`

### Option B: Finish the migration, clean up SID history, then filter
1. Re-permission resources that still rely on old SIDs.
2. Clear sIDHistory from migrated accounts.
3. Apply Option A.

### PowerShell
```powershell
Import-Module ActiveDirectory

# Inspect the trust (TrustAttributes 0x40 = SID history enabled on a forest trust;
# SIDFilteringQuarantined = quarantine on an external trust)
Get-ADTrust -Filter "Target -eq '<TrustedDomain>'" |
    Select-Object Name, Direction, TrustType, TrustAttributes, SIDFilteringQuarantined, SIDFilteringForestAware

# Find accounts that still carry SID history
Get-ADUser -Filter * -Properties SIDHistory | Where-Object { $_.SIDHistory } |
    Select-Object SamAccountName, SIDHistory

# Forest trust: turn SID history off (elevated prompt, DC of the trusting domain)
netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /enablesidhistory:no

# External trust: quarantine it (elevated prompt, DC of the trusting domain)
netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /quarantine:yes
```

## Verify
- Forest trust: `netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /enablesidhistory` reports
  that SID history is disabled, and
  `(Get-ADTrust -Filter "Target -eq '<TrustedDomain>'").TrustAttributes -band 0x40` is 0.
- External trust: `netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /quarantine` reports that
  SID filtering is enabled.

Collect again (or use Retest on the finding) and confirm the
finding ID is gone.

## Rollback
- Forest trust: `netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /enablesidhistory:yes`.
- External trust: `netdom trust <TrustingDomain> /domain:<TrustedDomain> /quarantine:no`.

Either restores the previous behavior. Do this only temporarily, and accept the finding as a risk
in Settings, with a review date.
